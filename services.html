The Consultation Journey

How We Bring Your Vision to Life

A seamless, personalized four-step process from initial showroom discovery to white-glove in-home installation.

01

Discovery & Space Mapping

We review your floorplans, architectural aesthetic, light exposure, and daily lifestyle requirements to define spatial priorities.

02

Material & Fabric Curation

Explore tangible wood swatches, full-grain leathers, and bouclé textiles in our studio to establish a warm, tactile color palette.

03

Custom Crafting & Inspection

Our artisan workshops assemble and finish each selected item, followed by a rigorous 24-point quality and stability inspection.

04

White-Glove Installation

Our dedicated delivery crew transports, unboxes, positions, and cleans every piece in your home, removing all packaging materials.
